## Competitors

Jobscan, Huntr and JobCopilot match on CV wording or automate application volume. None explains whether a low score means poor fit or missing profile data.

## Positioning

Competitors tell you how well your CV matches the ad's wording; CareerPilot tells you whether you fit the job, and why.
